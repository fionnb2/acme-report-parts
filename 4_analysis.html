Executive Summary & Analysis
Revenue Performance
Net revenue data spans four fiscal years across all quarters, measured in millions USD. The chart illustrates significant volatility, with values ranging from a low of 2,881 M USD to a peak of 8,033 M USD.
Peak & Decline
Revenue surged to its four-year high of 8,033 M USD in Q4 of 2024 before experiencing a sharp correction. 2023 saw consistent year-over-year contraction of approximately –2.8% to –3.0% per quarter.
Recovery Pattern
2025 Q1 posted the strongest YoY recovery at +18.4%, followed by 2026 Q1 at +20.2%, suggesting early-period seasonal strength. 2024 Q1 and Q2 also showed positive momentum at +8.5% and +9.1%.
Recent Trend
2026 Q2 marked a multi-year trough at 2,881 M USD. Subsequent quarters show a gradual recovery to 3,578 M (Q3) and 3,782 M (Q4), indicating a nascent stabilisation phase that warrants continued monitoring.
